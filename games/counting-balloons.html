About this game

Made for toddlers (with a grown-up nearby): numbered balloons — however many you choose, up to 10 — are scattered around the screen in a new arrangement each round. Tapping the next number in sequence pops it with a cheerful chime and says the number out loud; tapping out of order just gives the balloon a gentle wobble, no penalty, no losing. Popping them all starts a fresh round with the balloons shuffled to new spots. Uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis to say each number.
