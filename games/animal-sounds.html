About this game

Made for babies and toddlers (with a grown-up nearby): an animal sound plays out loud, and the child taps the animal that makes it among six options. A correct match pops and celebrates with the animal's name; a miss just gently shakes so they can try again — nothing is ever wrong for long. Uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis to say each sound word.
