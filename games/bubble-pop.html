About this game

Made for babies and toddlers (with a grown-up nearby): big bubbles, a gentle pop sound, and nothing to fail at. Bubbles keep coming on their own — there's no timer and nothing to win or lose. The speed slider sets the base pace, and mashing "Speed up!" ramps it up further each time for older kids who want more chaos — "Reset speed" brings it back to normal.
