About this game

In Baby mode (the default — made for babies and toddlers with a grown-up nearby), bubbles that float away unpopped don't matter — nothing to fail at, ever. Switch to Challenge mode and missing just one ends the round, for older kids who want an actual game. Your score only counts while the tab stays open — closing it loses an unfinished round, but your best score is saved on this device. The speed slider sets the base pace, and mashing "Speed up!" ramps it up further each time — "Reset speed" brings it back to normal.
