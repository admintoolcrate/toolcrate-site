About this game

Red moves first. Click a piece, then click a highlighted square to move it. Captures are mandatory when available, and a piece that can jump again immediately must keep jumping. A man that reaches the far row becomes a king and can move diagonally in any direction. Whoever runs their opponent out of legal moves wins. Scores are saved on this device.
