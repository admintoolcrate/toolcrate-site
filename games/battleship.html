About this game

Standard 10×10 Battleship with the classic five-ship fleet: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), and Destroyer (2). Place your fleet manually or hit Randomize, then take turns firing one shot each — no bonus shot on a hit. Sink an opponent's entire fleet to win.

In pass-and-play mode, a hand-off screen appears between every placement and every turn so whoever currently has the device never sees the other player's board until they've actually handed it over.
