About this game

After clicking to start, the box turns red for a random delay (1–4 seconds), then green — click the instant it turns green. Clicking during the red phase counts as a false start and restarts the round without recording a time. Your best time and the average of your last 5 valid attempts are saved with localStorage on this device. Typical human visual reaction time is roughly 200–300ms; much faster than that usually means you anticipated the change rather than reacted to it.
