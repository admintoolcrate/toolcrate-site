Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

The tools on this site run entirely in your browser. Whatever you type into a converter, formatter, or counter is processed on your own device and is never sent to our servers, because we don't have any servers to send it to — this is a static site.

What we don't collect

We don't require accounts, logins, or email addresses. We don't store the text, numbers, or files you enter into any tool.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by third-party networks (such as Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads and measure performance. This is standard for ad-supported sites and is governed by the ad network's own privacy policy, not ours directly — see Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads for details on how they handle this.

You can control or opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings or via aboutads.info/choices.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic (e.g. how many people visit a page), without identifying individual visitors personally.
