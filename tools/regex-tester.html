About this tool

This tester runs your pattern using JavaScript's native RegExp engine, so behavior matches exactly what you'd get in browser JavaScript or Node.js — flavors like PCRE (used in PHP or many online tools) can differ slightly in edge cases. Common flags: g finds all matches instead of stopping at the first, i ignores case, m makes ^ and $ match line boundaries instead of just the start/end of the whole string, and s lets . match newlines.

Everything runs locally in your browser — the pattern and text you enter are never sent anywhere.
