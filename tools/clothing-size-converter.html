About this tool

This follows commonly published general size charts for women's and men's clothing. Clothing sizing is the least standardized of all the sizing systems on this site — it varies not just by country but by garment type, cut, and brand (a US size 8 dress and a US size 8 pair of jeans aren't guaranteed to fit the same body the same way), so treat this as a general starting point for comparing size labels across regions, not a precise fit guarantee.

Nothing you select here is sent to a server — the entire tool runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.
