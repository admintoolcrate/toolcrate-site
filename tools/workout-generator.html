    How this works

    Exercises are drawn from a curated pool tagged by goal and equipment, then picked at random each time you generate — so two workouts with the same settings won't always match. Sets, reps, and durations scale with the difficulty you choose (or a sensible intermediate default in simple mode). Rest between sets is a suggestion, not a strict timer built into this tool.

    This isn't personalized training advice — it's a randomizer to take the "what should I do today" decision off your plate. Adjust anything that doesn't suit your fitness level or any injuries, and stop any exercise that causes pain. Your last-used settings are saved in your browser (nothing is sent to a server) so they're remembered next time.
