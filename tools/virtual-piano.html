About this tool

Notes are generated with the Web Audio API — real oscillators, not audio file playback — spanning C4 to E5. Click or tap a key, or use the mapped computer keys (A S D F G H J K L ; for the white keys, W E T Y U O P for the black keys in between) to play, including holding multiple keys down for chords.

Nothing here is recorded or uploaded — sound is generated and played entirely in your browser.
