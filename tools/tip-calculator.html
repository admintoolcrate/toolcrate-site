The formula

tip = bill × (percent ÷ 100), and total = bill + tip. Splitting divides both the tip and the total evenly by the number of people — it doesn't account for anyone ordering more or less than the group average.

Nothing entered here is sent to a server — the entire calculation runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.
