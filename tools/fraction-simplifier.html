Fraction Simplifier

Reduces any fraction to its lowest terms.

Ad slot — in-content

How this works

Both numerator and denominator are divided by their greatest common divisor (GCD) to reach the smallest equivalent whole-number fraction.

Example: 18/24 shares a GCD of 6, simplifying to 3/4.
