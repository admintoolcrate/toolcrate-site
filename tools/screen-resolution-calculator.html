About this tool

The aspect ratio is the width and height divided by their greatest common divisor — 1920×1080 shares a GCD of 120, reducing to the familiar 16:9. "Common name" matches against widely-used ratios (16:9, 4:3, 21:9, 16:10, 3:2, 1:1, 5:4) within a small tolerance, since real-world resolutions don't always reduce to a perfectly clean fraction.

Nothing you enter here is sent to a server — the entire tool runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.
