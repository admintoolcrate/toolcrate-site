About this estimate

The baseline is a commonly cited rule of thumb — about 35 mL per kg of body weight — plus roughly 12 mL for every minute of exercise (in the range of the 350–700 mL per 30–60 minutes often recommended for moderate activity), plus a flat 500 mL if you're in a hot or humid climate where sweat losses run higher. This includes water from food and other drinks, not just what you drink from a glass, and it's a starting point, not a target to hit exactly every day.
