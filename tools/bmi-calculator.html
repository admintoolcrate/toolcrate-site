The formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². The World Health Organization's adult categories are: under 18.5 = underweight, 18.5–24.9 = normal weight, 25–29.9 = overweight, 30 and above = obese. These ranges were developed from population-level data and are a rough screening tool, not a diagnosis — talk to a healthcare provider for an assessment that accounts for your individual body composition.
