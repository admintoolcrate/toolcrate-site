Percentage of a Number

Find out what X% of Y is. Type either value and the result updates instantly.

The formula

result = (percentage ÷ 100) × number

For example, 15% of 200 is (15 ÷ 100) × 200 = 30. This is the calculation behind tips, discounts, taxes, commissions, and most everyday percentage questions.
