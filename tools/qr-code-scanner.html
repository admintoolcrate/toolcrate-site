About this tool

Decoding happens entirely in your browser using the open-source jsQR library — the uploaded image or camera frame is never sent anywhere. Upload a photo or screenshot containing a QR code, or use your camera to scan one live if your browser and device support it (this needs a secure context and camera permission).

Nothing you scan is stored or transmitted; if the decoded content is a link, it's shown as plain text — always check a URL before opening it, the same as you would with any QR code from an unknown source.
