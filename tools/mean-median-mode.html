Definitions

Mean is the sum divided by the count. Median is the middle value when sorted (or the average of the two middle values for an even count). Mode is the most frequently occurring value. Range is the highest value minus the lowest. Standard deviation here is the population standard deviation, measuring how spread out the values are from the mean.
