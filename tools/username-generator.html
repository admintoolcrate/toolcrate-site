About this tool

Each username pairs a random adjective with a random noun from a built-in word list of about 100 of each, optionally followed by a 2 or 3-digit number to further reduce collisions. With both lists this size and a number suffix, there are well over a million possible combinations — plenty for picking a handle, though it's not intended as a substitute for checking whether a specific name is actually available on the platform you're using it for.

Nothing generated here is sent to a server — the entire tool runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.
