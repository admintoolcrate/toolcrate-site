About this tool

Each day's hours are clock-out minus clock-in, minus any unpaid break, with overnight shifts (clock-out earlier than clock-in) automatically treated as crossing midnight. Overtime is calculated on the week as a whole, past the threshold you set — the default of 40 hours/week and 1.5× pay matches the U.S. federal standard, but adjust both fields to match your own timesheet rules.

Nothing entered here is sent to a server — the entire calculation runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.
