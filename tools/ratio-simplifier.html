Ratio Simplifier

Reduces any ratio to its lowest whole-number terms.

Copied

How this works

The ratio is simplified by dividing both numbers by their greatest common divisor (GCD). For decimal inputs, both values are first scaled up to whole numbers before simplifying.

Example: 24:36 shares a GCD of 12, so it simplifies to 2:3.
