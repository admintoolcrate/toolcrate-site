About this tool

The Gregorian calendar repeats its weekday pattern on a 400-year cycle, so this works the same way for a date centuries in the past or future as it does for next week — there's no special-casing, just calendar-day arithmetic done consistently with UTC dates so the result doesn't depend on your time zone.

Nothing entered here is sent to a server — the entire calculation runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.
