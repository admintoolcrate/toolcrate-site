How this counts

Words are counted as whitespace-separated tokens. Sentences are estimated by counting terminal punctuation (., !, ?), so abbreviations may occasionally inflate the count slightly. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute, a common average for adult silent reading.

Everything is calculated locally in your browser as you type — nothing is sent anywhere.
