What Percent Is X of Y?

Find out what percentage one number represents of another.

Copied

The formula

percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

For example, 30 is what percent of 120? (30 ÷ 120) × 100 = 25%. Useful for grades, survey results, completion rates, and comparing a part to a whole.
