The formulas

Simple interest: A = P × (1 + r × t)

Compound interest: A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t), where n is the number of times interest compounds per year.

This is a simplified educational model — it doesn't account for taxes, fees, variable rates, or additional contributions over time.
