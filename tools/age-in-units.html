About this tool

Each figure is the same span of time expressed in a different unit — total hours is just total days × 24, and so on — measured against your device's clock and updated every second. Without a birth time, the count starts from midnight on your birth date, so the totals will be off by up to 24 hours from your literal birth moment.

Nothing entered here is sent to a server — the entire calculation runs as JavaScript in your browser tab, and stops updating if you close or leave the page.
