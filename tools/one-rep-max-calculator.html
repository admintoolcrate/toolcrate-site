The formulas

Epley: 1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). Brzycki: 1RM = weight × 36 ÷ (37 − reps) — this one is undefined at 37 reps and unreliable well before that, so treat any high-rep estimate from either formula as a rough ballpark rather than a number to load on the bar.
