About this tool

Standard violin tuning (low to high: G D A E), synthesized as a sustained tone with a light vibrato while held, rather than a plucked or percussive sound — a real violin has no frets, so this is a simplified 12-position layout for playability rather than a literal fingerboard.

Nothing here is recorded or uploaded — sound is generated and played entirely in your browser.
