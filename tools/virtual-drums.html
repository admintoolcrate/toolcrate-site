About this tool

Each pad is synthesized rather than sampled: the kick is a low sine tone with a fast pitch drop, the snare and hi-hats are filtered noise bursts of different lengths and brightness, and the toms/cymbal follow the same idea at different pitches and decay times. It's a reasonable approximation of a drum kit's character, not a sampled recording.

Nothing here is recorded or uploaded — sound is generated and played entirely in your browser.
