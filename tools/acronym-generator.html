About this tool

Each word's first letter is taken in order to build the acronym. "Skip small words" leaves out a short list of common articles, conjunctions, and prepositions (a, an, the, of, in, on, at, for, and, or, but, to, with) the way many real-world acronyms do — "As Soon As Possible" becomes ASAP either way here since none of its words are in that list, but a phrase like "State of the Union" would become SU instead of SOTU with it on.

Nothing you type here is sent to a server — the entire tool runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.
