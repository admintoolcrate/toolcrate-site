About this tool

Standard tuning (low to high: E A D G B E), 12 frets per string, synthesized with the Web Audio API using a short, decaying plucked-string envelope rather than a sampled recording. It's an approximation of a guitar's timbre, not a modeled or sampled instrument.

Nothing here is recorded or uploaded — sound is generated and played entirely in your browser.
