Support toolcrate

Keep the tools free and the ads to a minimum.

Every tool on this site is free, runs entirely in your browser, and doesn't ask you to sign up. Hosting and keeping it running still costs something, so we run a light, standard ad or two — kept to a minimum on purpose, rather than the pop-ups and autoplay video you'll find on most sites like this.

If a tool has saved you some time and you'd like to help keep it that way, a donation goes directly toward covering costs and reducing how much we need to lean on ads. It's entirely optional — every tool stays free either way.
