How this works

Toolcrate has no server or database, so there's no contact form to submit to — "Open in email app" simply builds a pre-filled email (to admin.toolcrate@gmail.com) and hands it to whatever email app your browser or device has set up, the same as clicking a normal mailto: link. If that doesn't open anything (common if you don't have a default email app configured on this device), use "Copy email address" and send it from wherever you do check mail.

Nothing you type here is sent anywhere by this page itself — it only ever leaves your device once you actually send the email.
