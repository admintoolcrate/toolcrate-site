About Toolcrate

Small tools, built to do one job well.

What this is

Toolcrate is a growing collection of small, single-purpose tools — calculators, converters, formatters, and generators — built for anyone who needs a quick, reliable answer without wading through ads, pop-ups, or unrelated content to get it.

How it works

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. There's no account to create, no data sent to a server, and nothing saved after you close the tab. That's a deliberate choice: these are meant to be fast, disposable utilities you can bookmark and return to, not products that ask anything of you beyond solving the problem in front of you.

Why it exists

Most single-purpose tools online are buried under intrusive pop-ups, autoplay video, or a wall of content before you reach the actual tool. Toolcrate keeps things simple: a light, standard ad or two to keep the lights on, and the tool itself front and center — nothing else competing for your attention.

Accuracy

Care is taken to get formulas and reference values right, and sources are noted where relevant. That said, no tool here should be treated as professional advice — see the Terms of Use for the full disclaimer, especially for anything health, financial, or safety-related.
