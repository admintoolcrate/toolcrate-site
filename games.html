About these games

Every game runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to make. High scores are saved with localStorage, so they're specific to this browser on this device: they won't follow you to a different browser or computer, and clearing your browser data clears them too. There's no global leaderboard (that would need a server, which this site deliberately doesn't have).
